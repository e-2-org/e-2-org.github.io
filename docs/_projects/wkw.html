Culture, however you define it, is full of often delightfully unanticipated connections - links of friendship, collaboration or sometimes antagonism - between interesting individuals. But where do you go if you want to focus on these and find out more?

Who Knew Whom could be the roadmap you didn’t know you needed: an emergent atlas of individuals and their significant links, a place to go for random stimulus to lead the way. Who Knew Whom: cultural cartography on your desktop.
