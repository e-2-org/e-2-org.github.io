e-2 was invited by Ian Ritchie Architects to research and write a feasibility study for the London Borough of Hammersmith & Fulham, suggesting an arts strategy for Shepherds Bush Green. The Green will be completely re-landscaped by 2005, and e-2's report outlines ideas for an innovative use of this outdoor urban space.
