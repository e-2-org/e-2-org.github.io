Ian Ritchie Architects commissioned e-2 to develop a site for their internationally renowned architectural practice, to represent works ranging from the small to large-scale such as the award-winning Jubilee Line Bermondsey Station.

e-2 developed a site which is easy to navigate, and yet holds a great deal of material. The site has 'vaults' which hold information about each project including architectural drawings and plans, and 'threads' which enable visitors to search for subjects in a non-linear way through the site.

The site has been redeveloped three times since 2000:

  • 2000 (original)
  • 2010 (blue)
  • 2016 (handwritten)
  • ritchie.studio (current site)
