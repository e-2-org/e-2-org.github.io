Notion Nanny was a touring project in which the artist Allison Smith took on the role of an itinerant apprentice, travelling through rural and urban districts in search of traditional skills and revolutionary dialogue. Through residencies and exchanges she built a temporary guild, a platform for discussion and a sculptural installation, which together formed a travelling exhibition, with stops in Cumbria, London, Shropshire, North Adams, Massachusetts and Berkeley, California.

e-2 produced the project’s website (notionnanny.net) with the artist, who used it, along with blogger.com and flickr, to communicate with the people she met along the way. It is archived here as it was in 2010.
