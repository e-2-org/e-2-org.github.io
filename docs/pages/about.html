e-2 worked in partnership with artists and arts organisations, to research, advise on, create and support projects for new media, new platforms and new spaces.

Established in 1997 to explore the creative and cultural potential of emerging digital technologies, e-2 has commissioned and presented a series of substantial new works from up and coming contemporary artists, three of whom went on to achieve a Turner Prize nomination.

The works have made a major contribution to developing the potential of digital media as a significant site for contemporary art and culture, challenging accepted views of art, its contexts, and boundaries.

e-2 has also provided web consultancy and production services to arts organisations and major galleries, including Tate Modern, Tate Britain, Royal Academy of Arts, Beaconsfield, Chisenhale Gallery, Camden Arts Centre, and to many artists on individual projects.
