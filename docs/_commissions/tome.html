A virtual world where a series of rooms are described partly through 3D modelling and partly through prose. A contemplation upon the fragility of memory, showing the viewer the artist’s past as depicted by the living spaces he once inhabited.

The piece was made in VRML, for the Cosmo Player plugin. It now plays in the browser, with no plugin.
