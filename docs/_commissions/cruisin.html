Cruisin’ was a commission for e-2's previous incarnation as c-ship. Collins’ site-specific piece inhabits the 'ship' with a chat space which roams the decks. Cruisin’ explores issues of interaction in digital space and, by its addictive nature, reveals how we yearn for human interaction in this dislocated realm.

Unfortunately Cruisin’ is unavailable at the moment. If you would like to be notified when it is back online please contact us.
