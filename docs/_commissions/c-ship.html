Container-ship (1998) was e-2’s first commissioning programme, and e-2’s name at the time: an innovative digital arts project, commissioning new work from contemporary artists specifically for the Internet. It was commissioned by the Japanese Internet arts organisation NMP (Network Museum Project), curated by Simon Faithfull and produced by Peter Moore.

The site is a ship. Its rooms hold the ideas behind the project, and its containers hold the eight works it commissioned: error 404 by Anna Best, now here/nowhere by Brighid Lowe, tome by David Lilley, a house is not a home by Geraint Evans, pig’s tails by Gill Ord, Bird Island by Janice Kerbel, Cruisin’ by Susan Collins and Adelaide by Simon Faithfull.
