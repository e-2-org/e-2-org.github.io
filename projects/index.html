e-2 is one of the UK's leading digital arts organisations, developing innovative projects within new media, new platforms and new spaces. e-2 works closely with artists and cultural organisations, providing resources in creative consultancy, research, production and commissioning - often in partnership.

e-2 takes on challenging and interesting projects ranging from offering advice, research and support to individual artists through the complex process of creating a new work to be made or exhibited using digital technology; undertaking research and feasibility on new projects for the visual arts; large scale organisational consultancy and complete redesign for large, complex websites. e-2 provides complete, advanced technical production and support, from hosting and internet server set-up through to implementation of database functionality, content management systems, data capture and statistics.
